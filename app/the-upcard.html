The Upcard

Play blackjack with a coach that flags your mistakes and a strategy chart you can study.

v0.7.1, complete · since August 2026 · one file

Play blackjackSee it on the phone

Why I made it

I shared my card games with a friend and his girlfriend, and over the next few days he kept telling me he was just playing the blackjack one, over and over. So I decided to make that game complete and then build a whole training system around it. You can just play blackjack, alone or with others, but wrapped around the table is a coach and a strategy chart designed to make you a better player and teach you to read the cards in front of you. It is the blackjack version of Knight School: play and learn in one app.

How it came together

Built in four phases plus splits, done at v0.7 on Aug 27. Core basic strategy for six decks, dealer stands on 17. The chart cells are generated from the same engine the coach uses, so the two can never disagree, and the engine was verified 270 for 270 against the canonical chart. Chips, a $500 bankroll that resets on bust, manual sessions, and separate tracking for money and accuracy with growth charts. Teal felt, orange dealer arc, and a card back with the site's aperture mark. No card counting.

What I learned

Generating the teaching chart from the engine instead of typing it in was the right call. Anything typed twice drifts.
