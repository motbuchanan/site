Star Stuff

Drop and merge planets around a point of gravity, climbing from stardust all the way to a galaxy.

Playable, still growing · one file

Play Star StuffSee it on the phone

Why I made it

My son was enjoying a merge game with an ocean theme where the pieces floated up to the surface to merge, the opposite of every other game in the style, where they drop to the bottom. That made me want to play with gravity. Space was always going to be the theme, and I wanted the pieces to have personality: gentle and cozy, planets that squash, bounce and react when they collide.

How it came together

Early on you could choose which way gravity pulled: up, down, or at any angle toward a wall or a corner, and each direction made the planets collide and merge differently. That survives as Arcade mode. Then I put a black hole in the center and had everything fall toward it, and the unexpected consequence was that the pile starts to rotate on its own as you keep adding to it, so you are aiming at a moving target. Merges earn stars to spend on boosts, upgrades, modes and skins. The Surge boost went from three seconds to ten with stacking after playtests showed three was too short to matter. It was live on the site on day one.

What I learned

The best mechanic in the game was an accident. I did not design the rotation, I noticed it and kept it. The danger ring needs to leave real room for big planets or the game is unfair, and a face rig that is basic reads as lifeless, so each tier needs its own character.
