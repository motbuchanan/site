Why I made it

This one was purely for me. I enjoy sudoku and already had a couple on my phone, so I just wanted to build my own, with a daily everyone in the house could share.

How it came together

The first version was one of the early site games. It got a new engine on Aug 20 that generates fresh puzzles at four levels, with a daily puzzle and streak, pencil marks, hints and undo.

What I learned

The rebuild was the lesson. The first pass was not something I was proud of, so I replaced the engine.
