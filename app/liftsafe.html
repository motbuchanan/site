LiftSafe

A forklift operator course from OSHA and NIOSH sources: pick your truck, learn to inspect, fuel and drive it.

v3.0.5, complete · one file

Start trainingSee it on the phone

Why I made it

A couple of people I work with needed to become certified forklift operators, and I wanted to give them something to play around with and practice scenarios on, so the knowledge was already there when the real test came. I also wanted to see whether publicly available information alone could make a real training tool, not a reskin of some vendor manual. Then someone needed to learn a propane truck specifically, and the app grew to ask which truck you are on and tailor everything to it.

How it came together

It is brand-neutral and sourced from OSHA 1910.178 and 1910.110, NIOSH 2001-109 and ANSI B56.1, plus published training material from equipment dealers, with a sources screen that says so. Thirteen modules and a quiz, a stability triangle you can drag, and a small driving simulator with a direction lever, gas pedal and rear-wheel steering built into the driving module. Version 2.3 turned the do and don't lists into animations: load handling, tail swing, startup, propane cylinder change, travel height. Version 3 added a trainer area with a hands-on evaluation checklist and a printable record.

What I learned

It is not a certification and it says so everywhere. An accuracy audit against the sources caught a real error in my tip-over statistics, and fixing the tip-over animation so it read as a sideways rollover instead of a wheelie took three tries.
