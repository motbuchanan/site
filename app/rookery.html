A free checklist for the complete Hallmark Beauty of Birds ornament line: the main yearly series, the Lady companions, the miniatures, the special editions, the premium and seasonal Keepsakes, and the branded tie-in sets. Check off what you own, count your doubles, and tap any bird to look it up or see it on eBay. There is a money tab too, for anyone who buys and resells to help fund the collection, and everything stays on your own phone.

Why I made it

My wife and I collect these. We wanted one place to see what we already have and what we are still chasing, instead of a photo of a spreadsheet. The resale side came from how we actually grow the collection: sell the doubles to pay for the rarer ones, and never have to wonder whether it is still paying for itself.

How it came together

One HTML file, saved on your device, nothing to sign up for. The list was researched against Hallmark’s own dreambook and the collector catalogs, eighty-plus pieces from 2005 to this year, and it is editable so you can add each new bird as it is announced. The money tab splits a lot’s cost evenly across the birds in it, folds in shipping and fees, and tells you whether your sales are covering your buying.

What I learned

Getting the list right was the real work. Hallmark drops retired ornaments from their own site, the miniatures and companion pieces are barely documented anywhere, and two years were mislabeled across the sources I checked. I ended up cross-checking the official dreambook against three seller catalogs to pin the exact years. For a collector tool, the data is the product, not the interface.
