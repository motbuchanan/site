Why I made it

I enjoy Wordle and wanted to understand the mechanics behind it a little better. It was a curiosity build first. I also wanted a daily word the whole house gets the same version of, with a streak that only the daily counts toward.

How it came together

Shipped Jul 19. 1,038 curated answers, which is about 2.8 years of dailies. The daily word is derived from the date so everyone sees the same one. Guesses are checked against a 168,559-word list I already had, so it added no download. Duplicate-letter scoring was verified against five hard cases. Redesigned Aug 16 from the layout up so it fits every phone.

What I learned

The curiosity build turned into a lesson about word lists: where to find a good one, how to curate answers separately from valid guesses, and how much a list can weigh. Duplicate letters are where word games go wrong, so I tested those cases before anything else.
