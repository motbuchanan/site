All apps

Everything on my phone-shaped home screen, as a plain list. Every one is free and runs in your browser. I built them all on a phone.

Play

  • Pocket SharkA 3D pool table you can walk around, with a shark that reads the table and shows you the best shot. 8-ball, 9-ball, a story-mode hustle up the ladder, and Shark Eyes for real tables.
  • The KeepA living valley drawn in letters. Walk it, meet the people who live there, then take the road out to the next one.
  • Star StuffDrop and merge planets around a point of gravity, climbing from stardust all the way to a galaxy.
  • Brick BreakerDrag the paddle, keep the ball alive, grab the power-ups, chain combos.
  • SudokuFresh puzzles at four levels, a daily with a streak, pencil marks, hints and undo.
  • Game ShelfEleven board games and thirteen card games on one wooden shelf. Tap a box to play the computer or pass the phone.
  • JigsawTurn any photo on your phone, or one you take right now, into a jigsaw with your choice of piece count and cut.
  • Five LettersGuess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. One daily word for everyone, plus unlimited practice.

Learn

  • The Brew DeckA homebrew training system. Read recipes whose strength, bitterness and colour are calculated live from the ingredients, play a brew day as a series of decisions and watch each choice move the beer, then build your own recipe from what you have on hand and get it graded against the style.
  • Knight SchoolLearn chess from square one on a real board, then play the computer at ten levels with a coach watching.
  • All ThumbsA build-it-on-a-phone course with interactive explainers, sandboxes, drills and quizzes for total beginners.
  • SHARPAn on-screen piano that lights up the next key. Tap to hear notes, listen to a song, then play it back.
  • HARPA ten-hole harmonica trainer: blow is orange, draw is teal, follow the glowing hole and learn riffs and songs.
  • The UpcardPlay blackjack with a coach that flags your mistakes and a strategy chart you can study.
  • Measure UpA music game that teaches math, reading, spelling, science, geography and more. You lead the band.

Make

  • RoughCutA real video editor that runs in your phone browser. Import clips, trim and reorder on a touch timeline, lay music and titles under them, add transitions (dip, crossfade, slide, wipe, zoom), animate text in real fonts with an outline or a glow, and export a finished MP4. Nothing uploads. The whole edit and the encode happen on your device, on Android and iPhone alike.
  • PigmentA giant color wheel to find and name colors, mix them like paint or light, play color games, and keep your own.
  • NazcaAn infinite drawing canvas you can zoom into forever, with kid brushes, pro tools, layers, and replay.
  • BloomTurn the dials, pick the colors, and grow a different real-looking plant every time. Tap the canvas to plant where you touch.
  • Paper WingTen paper airplanes with animated fold-by-fold steps, why each one flies, and a flight log.

Tool

  • Light ScoutA sun planner for photos. Tap a spot on the map, pick a day and a time, and see where the sun will be, which way the shadows fall and when golden hour starts. Plan a shot lines the sun up with a gap between buildings and lists every evening it happens.
  • RookeryA free checklist for the complete Hallmark Beauty of Birds ornament line: the main yearly series, the Lady companions, the miniatures, the special editions, the premium and seasonal Keepsakes, and the branded tie-in sets. Check off what you own, count your doubles, and tap any bird to look it up or see it on eBay. There is a money tab too, for anyone who buys and resells to help fund the collection, and everything stays on your own phone.
  • Civic CompassA pocket civic toolkit: election dates with reminders, who represents you from city hall to Congress, a voting plan, and tools to help you canvass or organize. Nonpartisan, and nothing leaves your phone.
  • Side BossA game that turns growing a side hustle into quests, levels and boss fights. Plan your week, log every sale and cost, and clear milestones like your first sale and your first thousand dollars.
  • GatherA holiday and party planner that lives on one phone: your people, the gifts and budget, a Secret Santa draw, who brings what, and party games, in four themes.
  • ReframePaste expert writing, get jargon and blame flagged with plainer wording. Eight professions, all on-device.
  • The Production TriangleDrag a point around the good, fast, cheap triangle and watch chasing one corner cost you the other two.
  • LiftSafeA forklift operator course from OSHA and NIOSH sources: pick your truck, learn to inspect, fuel and drive it.
  • SpectraPlay a note, then capture the color, shape and movement you see with it. Spectra saves every session and scores whether the same note always looks the same to you.
  • Two CansA private line between two phones. Text, photos, voice notes, doodles and eight turn-based games, locked so only two people can ever get in. This copy is the practice version: pick a side, any password works, and nothing leaves your phone.
