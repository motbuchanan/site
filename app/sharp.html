Why I made it

My son was starting to learn piano on our keyboard, using an app on his tablet as his first introduction, but it was limited unless you paid for a subscription. I wanted to build him something he could use at his own pace that goes past the basics into chords, ear training and practice habits. It also turned out to be a way for me to learn piano with just a phone in front of me.

How it came together

Two areas, Play and Woodshed. In Play, tap any key to hear it, pick a song and tap Listen to watch it once, then Practice and press each glowing key. Woodshed drills notes. Simple and Full modes, letter labels on the keys, octave shifting. It got a site brand pass at v0.2 with a recessed keybed and an orange felt strip after I flagged that press-only orange was invisible.

What I learned

Glowing keys teach faster than a staff. Songbook expansion with public-domain songs is still on the list.
