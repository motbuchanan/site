Why I made it

The family Game Shelf was already full of classic board and card games, so the next logical step was vintage video games. I started on the standards, Pong and Brick Breaker, and Brick Breaker was the one I focused in on first. I also wanted something simple that just works with a thumb, playable on the site the day it went live.

How it came together

Single-file canvas game with laser, multiball and extra-life power-ups and a combo counter. The build was a loop: play it, notice how it feels, describe what I want changed, play it again. That loop is how I learned to direct the AI on graphics, physics, power-ups and difficulty ramps. It was one of the two games live on launch day, Aug 14.

What I learned

Getting a paddle to feel right under a thumb is most of the game. And you cannot tune game feel from a description. You have to play it and then say what is wrong.
