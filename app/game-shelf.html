Why I made it

This whole site started on June 22 with a game shelf for my family. This is the public one: every board game and card game I have built, in one place, sitting on a shelf like the real thing.

How it came together

The shelf took over the Board Games and Card Games rooms. The chess engine and all thirteen card games moved onto it, and nine board games came over from the family shelf: backgammon, mancala, dominoes, Chinese checkers, dots and boxes, tic-tac-toe, snakes and ladders, shut the box and code breaker. The boxes are real 3D, and the lamp swings when you push it, dragging the light across the shelf.

What I learned

One shelf reads bigger than two rooms. Fewer doors, more games.
