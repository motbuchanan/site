Why I made it

Bloom was chasing curiosity. I saw something someone else had built that made me think of plants and flowers, and I wanted to see how close I could get to floral artwork using nothing but data. A seed you can turn into a hundred different plants, where the plant is grown from numbers instead of drawn.

How it came together

It is an L-system: sliders for fullness, spread, height, droop, flower size and density are the seed, and the plant grows one step per frame, forking at random times like fireworks and bursting into flower sprays at the tips. Color runs from a green stem up through purple to pink or teal tips, with front-and-back depth shading. Five presets, and you can save a frame as an image.

What I learned

One growth step per frame is what makes it feel alive; speeding that up just makes plants pop in fully formed. And blurring each segment for depth hung the whole render, so depth had to come from opacity instead.
