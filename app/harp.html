HARP

A ten-hole harmonica trainer: blow is orange, draw is teal, follow the glowing hole and learn riffs and songs.

Why I made it

I have been trying to teach myself harmonica for years. I am not musical in any way, but I love the harmonica, blues harmonica most of all, and there was no trainer I liked that fit on a phone. HARP is my latest attempt at finding a way to understand music, and it is the one that has stuck.

How it came together

Modes for Train, Songs, Free play, Jam and Listen. Train walks a blues road or a folk road: learn the major scale, three riffs, then a tune you know. Listen uses the mic so you play the glowing note on your real harp and it checks you. Bends are an advanced layer you add later. It is the sister app to SHARP and shares its look.

What I learned

Color-coding blow and draw is the whole trick. Once orange means out and teal means in, the tabs read themselves. I practice with this thing, and I can hear the improvement.
