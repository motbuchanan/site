The Production Triangle

Drag a point around the good, fast, cheap triangle and watch chasing one corner cost you the other two.

Why I made it

This came straight from a conversation on a production floor with someone who had never heard of the good-fast-cheap constraint. When I put this together and showed them, they got it instantly. That is the whole point: introduce the idea, plus the supply, demand and balance ideas around it, without tying it to any one industry. Shop-floor examples stay general on purpose. I expect this to be the first of many explainers like it, built whenever a conversation shows me one is missing.

How it came together

The core is a draggable point inside a triangle using barycentric coordinates, with the three values locked to a fixed budget of 100. Pull toward one corner and the other two drain. The page walks through the rule, the corners on the floor, the three deals, balance and demand, bending the triangle with lean, and a timeline of where the idea came from. It took ten versions on my phone to get the scroll-driven motion right.

What I learned

Scroll-linked reveals that move with your thumb feel right. One-shot fades that pop in late feel like an AI made them. Using svh instead of dvh for the sticky section fixed a jerky pin caused by the mobile toolbar hiding mid-scroll.
