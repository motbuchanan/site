Spectra

Play a note, then capture the color, shape and movement you see with it. Spectra saves every session and scores whether the same note always looks the same to you.

Why I made it

I wanted to find out whether what I see when I hear a sound is actually consistent, or whether I was imagining it. So I built an instrument to test myself: play a tone, log what I see, and let the numbers tell me if it holds up over weeks.

How it came together

It plays synthesized notes and lets you capture color, shape, movement and light for each one, then stores every session on your device. Four tabs: Capture, Notes, Calendar and Insights. It scores each capture against your first one for that note so you can watch for drift, lays the year out as a per-day spectrum, and can compare your captures against a friend export without ever touching your own scores. No account, no server, all on your phone.

What I learned

A consistency score turns a hunch into something you can actually check. And the whole thing is only worth anything if you keep coming back, so the hard part was making it take just a few taps to log a note.
