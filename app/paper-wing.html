Paper Wing

Ten paper airplanes with animated fold-by-fold steps, why each one flies, and a flight log.

v0.3, installable · since September 2026 · 6 files

Fold a planeSee it on the phone

Why I made it

My son and I were making paper airplanes for a class activity, and I realized I could explain the reasoning and the physics behind each design and make that part fun. So every plane comes with why it flies the way it does, and there is a flight log for testing distances, making an adjustment and throwing it again.

How it came together

The app folds a model of the sheet. It keeps the paper as a stack of layers, splits every layer along the crease, flips the moving half over and stacks it on top, so each step shows what your paper really looks like. Ten planes run on that one engine, from the classic Dart to a Ring that flies with no wings at all.

What I learned

Folding a model of the paper beat drawing every diagram by hand, and it caught mistakes a drawing would hide. A few steps still show as one layer seen from the top, so a real sheet next to the phone is still the best test.
