Jigsaw

Turn any photo on your phone, or one you take right now, into a jigsaw with your choice of piece count and cut.

Complete · since July 2026 · one file

Cut a puzzleSee it on the phone

Why I made it

It started when I realized I could reach the phone's camera from a web page and began thinking about what to do with that. My head was already in the activity-book corner of the Game Shelf, designing coloring pages, and I was trying to find images nice enough for that. Then it clicked that I could photograph things and modify them. Cutting a photo into jigsaw pieces was the first thing I tried. I also did not want every puzzle to have the same generic cut, so you choose the shape of the pieces.

How it came together

Built Jul 19. Cut styles are Classic, Straight, Wavy, Zigzag and Draw-it, where you draw a curve and it gets stamped on every join. A classic tab has a neck that folds back on itself, which a simple height curve cannot do, so each cut is a profile that is allowed to run backwards. Piece counts from 6 to 200, pieces pre-rendered so 200 build in about 70 milliseconds. Photos come from your library or the camera and are stored in IndexedDB because one phone photo is bigger than localStorage allows.

What I learned

Interlocking tabs need overhang. And a trace-your-own-regions mode, like photographing a US map and tracing all fifty states, is a different engine, so I deferred it rather than half-build it.
