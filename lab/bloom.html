How it works (take it)

Every plant is a rule that calls itself. A stem grows up a step, and each living tip has a random chance each frame to fork into 2 or 3 branches, thinner and a touch shorter. Those do the same. All tips advance together, so the whole thing grows at once.

Realism comes from the tips, not the trunk. When a branch runs out, it bursts into a spray of fine filaments each ending in a flower cluster. That's what makes baby's-breath read as baby's-breath instead of a bare tree.

Depth is fake but convincing. Some sprays are drawn dark and slightly blurred (behind), some bright and sharp (front). Your eye reads the light ones as closer. The color of each piece is mixed between your four pickers by how high and how deep it is.

The sliders are the seed. Fullness, angle, height, droop, flower size and density, plus the four colors, are all just numbers fed into that rule. Change them and you get lilac, fireweed, fern, whatever. No library, a couple hundred lines.
