How to play (5-card draw)

You and the bot each ante, then get 5 cards. Tap the cards you want to throw away (up to 3), then Draw to swap them for new ones.

After the draw there is one round of betting. Check to pass, Bet to put chips in. If someone bets, the other must Call to see the hands or Fold and give up the pot.

Best 5-card hand wins the pot: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card. Run a player out of chips to win the match.
