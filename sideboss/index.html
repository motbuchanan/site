How Side Boss works

It is your hustle, as a game

Every real move you make to grow your side hustle is a quest. Do it, mark it done, earn XP, level up. That is the whole thing.

The Board

Plan your week here. The board shows the moves that matter right now. Tap done when you have actually done one. No busywork, no fake tasks.

The Ledger

Log every sale and every cost. See your revenue, your profit, and your growth over the last six weeks. Logging your first sale finishes a quest on its own.

Boss battles

The big milestones are boss fights: your first sale, a hundred-dollar day, a thousand in the door, surviving the busy season. You do not grind them, you earn them by running the hustle. Beat one for a pile of XP and a piece of loot.

Shows, if you sell in person

Sell at markets or fairs? The Shows tab tracks each one: booth fee against what you sold there, so you can see which shows are worth your weekend. Turn it on any time from the Board with "Sell at markets?"

Level up and unlock seasons

XP raises your level and your five skills. Five seasons, timed to how a hustle grows: Getting Ready, First Sales, Find Your People, Busy Season, Level Up. Clear one to open the next.

It saves on this device

Your progress lives on this phone. Add it to your home screen so it is one tap away.
