About

Mot Buchanan
Designer and maker in Akron, Ohio.

This whole site is a phone. Every app on it, I built, most of them on a phone exactly like the one you're holding.

I like solving other people's puzzles: the thing they wish existed, the tool that would make their day easier, the game their kid would love. Then I build it.

The how: I direct a team of AI specialists from a phone. One builds, one tries to break it, one reviews it, and nothing ships until I say so. Knowing what to ask for, and what to throw away, is the craft.

Tap around. Everything here works. Take whatever is useful: every app's card has a link to its code.

Privacy

This phone doesn't track you.

No analytics. No cookies. No pixel following you around the internet later. Nothing you do here is recorded or sent anywhere.

The only thing saved is on your own device: which theme you picked, and whether you've seen the lock screen. That never leaves your phone.

The camera, if you open it, runs entirely on your device. No photo is uploaded, ever.
