All Thumbs

 

I build almost everything on a phone. This is the course I built to teach myself what was actually happening under it, one small explainer at a time.

Each book is a chapter with working sandboxes, missions, a practice gym and a five-question quiz. It fills with its color as you finish. Every gym has two tracks: General is the course anyone can take, and The Trail swaps in real cases from my own one-thumb phone builds. Tap a spine to pull it off the shelf.

How progress works: each chapter saves your missions and best quiz score in this browser, on this device. This page reads those saves live. Different device or browser, different progress. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
