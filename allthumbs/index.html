I build almost everything on a phone. This is the course I built to teach myself what was actually happening under it, one small explainer at a time.

Each part is a standalone interactive explainer with real working sandboxes, missions, a practice gym with randomized drills, and a five-question quiz. Do them in order for the full story: first how anything reaches your screen, then the toolbox people build it all with.

Every gym has a Your work level: drills shaped like the real thing I build with these tools, not textbook exercises. Supposedly all thumbs, I still built the whole thing. I saw what I needed to know, so I built the tool to teach it to myself.
