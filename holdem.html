How to play Hold'em

You and the bot each get 2 private cards. Five shared cards come out in the middle over three stages (flop, turn, river). Make your best 5-card hand from your 2 plus the 5.

There is a betting round before the flop and after each stage. Check or Bet when no one has bet; Call, Raise, or Fold when they have.

Best hand at the showdown wins the pot, or take it early when the other folds. Bust the bot to win.
