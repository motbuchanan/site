About this shelf

Where it started

The Game Shelf started as a home for the games I was building for my family. I build on my phone, one HTML file at a time. When someone at home asked for a game, it went on the shelf.

That shelf kept growing. It passed 50 games: board games, card games, old arcade games and activity books, plus player profiles, achievements and scores that sync between our devices. The family version is on build 62.

This shelf

This is the public cut. Eleven board games and a stack of card games, all old enough that nobody owns them. You play on one phone, against the computer or passed around the table. No accounts, no sign-in, and nothing you do here leaves your device.

The box art is still in progress. I'm making a cover for each game and swapping them in one at a time.
