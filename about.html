The short version
I went to art school to be an illustrator. I paid for it by twisting balloons and doing magic shows at birthday parties. After that I taught art for a few years, then came delivery trucks, a warehouse, and eventually running communications and a production lab. Somewhere in all of that I started building things with AI on my phone, and it turns out that's the thing I should have been doing all along. The things on this page are about old.
What I do now
I run communications and media production for a healthcare company. Video, print, web, social, brand, across ten locations. One person, all of it. I also ran the production floor for a few years, because someone needed to and I was already there.
How I build
Everything here is a single HTML file, designed and coded on a smartphone. No laptop, no framework, no team. Just a phone, the right questions, and a willingness to figure it out.
Why this exists
I like building things that work for real people. A phone and curiosity got me this far. Every app on this screen is that proof, and I'm not done adding to it. If that's interesting to you, say hi.
