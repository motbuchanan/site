## Locked decisions (do not reopen)
- CHROME.md is the standing chrome spec for every app on this site. New apps ship with it; edits to old apps keep it. (Sep 21)
- Tapping an icon opens its CARD, not the app. The card's verb button is the only launcher for grid apps. (Sep 21)
- Card copy is written in Mot's first-person voice, for a stranger. No employer, no coworkers, no job search, no son's name.
