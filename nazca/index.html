NAZCA

Draw a world inside a dot.

🏜️Why it is called Nazca

The Nazca lines are giant drawings scratched into a Peruvian desert two thousand years ago. On the ground they are furrows. From the sky they become a hummingbird, a monkey, a spider.

This app turns that inside out: the paper has no edges and no bottom. Zoom out and a lifetime of drawing becomes a speck. Zoom in and every line stays sharp, so you can draw a city inside an eye and a room inside a window. The inspiration is those videos that keep zooming into a picture and find another picture inside. Here you can make one.

✋Gestures
  • One finger draws. Two fingers move, zoom, rotate.
  • Two-finger tap undo. Three-finger tap redo.
  • Hold still at the end of a stroke: it snaps to a line, circle, or box.
  • Press and hold without moving: eyedropper.
  • Tap the canvas to close any panel.
🖌️Brush, Eraser, Fill
  • Brush: tap to draw with it, tap again to choose another. Pro, Fun, Alive, and Depth sections.
  • Eraser has its own size and softness.
  • Fill: tap inside a shape. "Close gaps" seals small openings; "Grow" hides the white fringe.
  • Size: size, opacity, hardness, Steady (smooths shaky hands), speed taper, symmetry.
  • Color: recent colors, saved palette, full wheel.
✂️Select, move, copy

Draw a loop around anything to lift it. Drag, pinch, rotate, then Done. Copy places a duplicate and keeps the piece. Seed turns it into a stamp brush.

After any stroke, the orange Adjust that line chip lifts it onto handles you can drag.

🧅Layers, references, tracing
  • Add, hide, reorder, merge, set opacity.
  • Reference image floats over the drawing; Trace image sits under it. Lock either in place.
  • Freeze moving things turns alive objects into pixels.
  • Shows from here / up to here: a layer that only appears between two zoom levels.
🔭Depth
  • The teal pill is the depth gauge. 1× is where you started; 512× is five hundred times closer. The paper is 16 million times deep.
  • Real-world scale: say the screen is 10 m wide and the gauge reads in meters, millimeters, light-years.
  • Depth map: where the deep work is. Tap to fly there.
  • Hidden note: words that appear only when you zoom in far enough.
  • Portal: a doorway that dives 100× deeper when you tap its name.
🎬Tours, replay, video
  • Tap the depth gauge for Tour. "+ Here" drops a keyframe. Tap keyframes to fly; arrows go start, previous, next, end.
  • Give a keyframe a caption and a hold time. Play tour flies through them. Record tour video saves that as a video, straight from the phone.
  • File → Replay drawing watches it draw itself; Export timelapse records that.
🚂Alive and fun

Wobbly crayon never sits still. Draw a train track and a train shows up. Bug trail puts bugs on any path. Fireworks go where you tap. Walking sticker sends any emoji pacing along a path. Grow sprouts leaves and flowers along a line over a few seconds. Fun brushes: crayon, rainbow, neon, glitter, bubbles, confetti, stickers.

💾Saving and sharing

Everything autosaves on this device. File → Export project makes one .nazca file with every layer, keyframe, note, and portal; open it on another phone. Save PNG exports what is on screen at the current depth, transparent and trimmed.

Nazca is a single file that runs in the browser. Made by Mot Buchanan in Akron, Ohio, mostly on a phone.
