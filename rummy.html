How to play Rummy

Make melds: a set is 3 or 4 cards of the same rank; a run is 3 or more in a row of the same suit (aces are low).

On your turn: draw one card (from the stock or the discard pile), lay down any melds you can, add cards to melds already on the table, then discard one card.

Go out by melding every card and discarding your last one. The other player's leftover cards count against them: face cards and tens are 10, aces are 1, everything else its number.

First to 100 points wins the match.
