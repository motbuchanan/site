How to play Hearts

Four players, 13 cards each. Each hand you pass 3 cards to another player, then play out 13 tricks. Follow the suit that was led if you can.

Avoid taking points: every heart is 1 point and the Queen of Spades is 13. Hearts cannot be led until one has been played on an earlier trick.

Take ALL 26 points and you "shoot the moon": you score 0 and everyone else takes 26. Lowest score when someone hits 100 wins.
