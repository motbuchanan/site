How to play Euchre

Four players in two teams (you + North). A 24-card deck (9 up to Ace). Everyone gets 5 cards and one card is turned up.

Pick trump: order the turned-up suit up, or if all pass, name a different suit. The Jack of trump is the right bower (highest); the other Jack of the same color is the left bower and also counts as trump.

Follow suit if you can. Highest trump wins a trick, else the highest card of the led suit. Make 3-4 tricks for 1 point, all 5 for 2, going alone and sweeping for 4. Get euchred and the other team scores 2. First to 10 wins.
