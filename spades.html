Spades

How to play Spades

You and North are partners against West and East. First, each player bids how many of the 13 tricks their side will take. Your two bids add together into a team target.

Spades are always trump. Follow the led suit if you can; if you cannot, you may play a spade to win. Spades cannot be led until one has been played on an earlier trick.

Make your team's bid for 10 points per trick bid. Miss it and you lose 10 per trick bid. Extra tricks are "bags," and every 10 bags costs 100. First side to 500 wins.
